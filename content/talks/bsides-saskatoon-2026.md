In this talk, I explored real-world experiences navigating security incident handling in high-stress environments, where customer impacts are real, and where it can feel like someone is always watching over my shoulder and judging my effectiveness. Autonomy is also commonly very low; I am expected to work magic, often without the ability to directly influence root causes and meaningful change. No two incidents are ever the same, and each requires a level of preparation and training I never feel I have enough time for. Due to these complexities, security operations and incident response is one of the highest stress positions in our industry, which is even more scary when I consider it's often the entry point for practitioners into cybersecurity.
