I took the audience on a journey that showed them how to be confident, creative, and capable when an incident is assigned to them. We showcased incident support structures like procedures, guidelines, and checklists, examining which ones work, which don't, and why. We also encouraged attendees to build their own and validate their effectiveness. Finally, we helped them identify focal points for getting the information they need, when they need it, so they can navigate this high-stress job with professionalism, dignity, and grace.
